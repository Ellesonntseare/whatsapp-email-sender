# Crownfall: Cannon Rush

A self-contained 3D arcade game. Aim the cannon, grow your crew through upgrade gates, and bring down three fortresses before time runs out.

Open [index.html](index.html) in a modern browser to play. Drag or use the left and right arrow keys to steer. The dotted aim guide shows where your next shot will travel, and the selected gate glows.

The Harbor Approach introduces gate choices. Crosswinds adds moving gates, hazards, and defenders that mark a lane before firing. The Citadel combines them with stronger defenses. Each stage has its own scenery and procedural music. The fortress cracks and loses battlements as it takes damage. Finish a level to unlock the next one; losing lets you retry that level.

The castle towers, doorway, pennants, and ballistae come from [Kenney's Castle Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit), licensed CC0. Selected source models and the original license are in [`assets/models`](assets/models). The playable HTML bundles the models and their texture so it can be opened directly.

The game includes Three.js and its GLTFLoader under the MIT license. The Three.js copyright and license notice are preserved in `index.html`.
