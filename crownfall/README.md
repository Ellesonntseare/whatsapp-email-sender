# Crownfall: Cannon Rush

A self-contained 3D arcade game. Aim the cannon, grow your crew through upgrade gates, and bring down the fortress before time runs out.

Open [index.html](index.html) in a modern browser to play. Drag or use the left and right arrow keys to steer.

The game includes Three.js under the MIT license. Its copyright and license notice are preserved in `index.html`.
